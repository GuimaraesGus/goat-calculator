# Goat Calculator

Two-player life point calculator for **Yu-Gi-Oh! Goat Format**. One phone lies in the middle of the table, and each half of the screen faces its player.

**Use it:** https://guimaraesgus.github.io/goat-calculator/

It runs in the browser, can be installed on the home screen, and works offline after the first visit.

## Features

- Two halves of the screen, each facing its player
- Calculator with automatic zeros (type 5 → 500) and Goat-era card shortcuts
- Counters and tokens with card details, for you and your opponent
- Dice, coin and random card picker
- Who-goes-first draw, best of 3, match timer and full history
- English, Portuguese and Spanish; light and dark themes

## Install on your phone

- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, tap the ⋮ menu, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

## Updating

Replace `index.html` with the new version and bump the cache name in `sw.js` (`goatcalc-v1` → `goatcalc-v2`). Installed copies pick up the update the next time they open with a connection.
