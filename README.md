## English

Free life point calculator for **Yu-Gi-Oh! Goat Format** duels. One phone lies between the two players, and each half of the screen faces its player. It runs in the browser, installs like an app and works offline.

### Install

| Phone | Browser | Steps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Android | Chrome | Menu ⋮ → **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**) |
| Android | Samsung Internet | Menu ☰ → **Add page to** → **Home screen** |
| iPhone | Safari | **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** |

Once installed, it opens full screen from the gold coin icon.

### Main screen

Each half shows, from its player's point of view:

- **Name and match score:** tap the name to edit it; two dots mark games won.
- **Tokens and counters:** the "+" button stays fixed on the left; the row scrolls sideways and shows arrows when there are more items.
- **Life points:** large number; tap it to open the calculator for your own life.
- **Opponent box:** your opponent's life and name; tap it to open the calculator for them. Their tokens sit just above it, with their own "+" fixed on the right.
- **History button** in the top corner, and the **timer** when it's on.

The center bar has undo (circular arrow), dice, coin, random card, end game (laurel wreath) and menu (gear). The icons mean the same thing from either side.

### Features

| Feature | How | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Life calculator | Tap your life or the opponent box | Pick the target at the top ("Name · you" or "Name · opponent"). The app suggests "00" in gray (5 becomes 500). Keys 00 and 000, delete ⌫ in amber, ½ for half your life. − and + preview the resulting life before you confirm. "Cancel" closes without changes. |
| Card shortcuts | Inside the calculator, applied to the chosen target | Premature Burial or Brain Control −800, Delinquent Duo −1000, Dimension Fusion or Injection Fairy Lily −2000, Cyber-Stein −5000, Snatch Steal +1000. Ring of Destruction deals the typed value to both players. Ceasefire: tap, type how many effect monsters are on the field, tap again (500 each). |
| Tokens and counters | "+" on your row (left) or the opponent's (right) | Counters and Tokens tabs. Each item shows the card's effect (tokens also show type, attribute, level, ATK and DEF) and its starting amount. In the row, tap to use; hold for −1, +1 and Remove. |
| Undo | Circular arrow in the bar | Reverts the last life or token change and tells you what was undone. Survives closing the app. |
| History | Button in each half | Opens facing whoever tapped it. Header with names and current life; "All" or "Life only" filter. Logs every change, plus dice, coins, random cards and the game start. The entry that decided a game is highlighted in the winner's color, with a "Victory" badge in the winner's column and a "Defeat" badge in the loser's. |

| Who goes first | When a duel starts | Dice (each player rolls 1 or 2 dice, highest total wins, ties reroll), coin (first to tap calls heads or tails) or "Skip draw"; "‹ Back" returns to the choice before anyone rolls or calls. Then "Duel!" and life counts up from 0 to 8000. |
| End game | Laurel wreath in the bar | Opens on both halves; each player chooses on their own side. **Win by Exodia** plays the "EXODIA, OBLITERATE!" animation and ends the game with "Obliterated by Exodia". **Win by Destiny Board** plays a spirit board animation spelling F-I-N-A-L (also shown when the Destiny Board counter reaches 5). **Win by Final Countdown** plays a 20-to-0 countdown dial ending with the app's eye (also shown when the Final Countdown counter runs out). **Concede** gives the win to the opponent. Both ask for confirmation; tap the screen to skip the animation. |
| Best of 3 | Automatic on a win, if on in the menu | Score at the top and a "Next game" button; the loser chooses who goes first; the timer covers the whole match. |
| Win screen | Life at zero, Destiny Board, Final Countdown, Exodia or a concession | "Victory!" or "Defeat" for each side, with reason and score; you can go back to the duel. |

## Português

Calculadora gratuita de pontos de vida para duelos de **Yu-Gi-Oh! no formato Goat**. O celular fica deitado entre os dois jogadores, e cada metade da tela fica virada para um deles. Roda no navegador, instala como app e funciona sem internet.

### Instalar

| Celular | Navegador | Caminho |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Android | Chrome | Menu ⋮ → **Instalar app** (ou **Adicionar à tela inicial**) |
| Android | Samsung Internet | Menu ☰ → **Adicionar página a** → **Tela inicial** |
| iPhone | Safari | **Compartilhar** → **Adicionar à Tela de Início** |

Depois de instalado, abre em tela cheia pelo ícone da moeda dourada.

### Tela principal

Cada metade mostra, do ponto de vista do seu jogador:

- **Nome e placar da partida:** toque no nome para editar; duas bolinhas marcam os jogos vencidos.
- **Tokens e contadores:** o botão "+" fica fixo à esquerda; a linha rola para o lado e mostra setas quando há mais itens.
- **Pontos de vida:** número grande; tocar abre a calculadora para a própria vida.
- **Quadro do oponente:** a vida e o nome do adversário; tocar abre a calculadora para ele. Logo acima ficam os tokens dele, com um "+" próprio fixo à direita.
- **Botão de histórico** no canto de cima e o **cronômetro**, quando ligado.

A barra central tem desfazer (seta circular), dado, moeda, carta aleatória, fim de jogo (coroa de louros) e menu (engrenagem). Os ícones têm o mesmo sentido vistos de qualquer lado.

## Español

Calculadora gratuita de puntos de vida para duelos de **Yu-Gi-Oh! en formato Goat**. El celular queda acostado entre los dos jugadores, y cada mitad de la pantalla mira hacia uno de ellos. Funciona en el navegador, se instala como app y funciona sin internet.

### Instalar

| Celular | Navegador | Pasos |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Android | Chrome | Menú ⋮ → **Instalar app** (o **Agregar a la pantalla principal**) |
| Android | Samsung Internet | Menú ☰ → **Añadir página a** → **Pantalla de inicio** |
| iPhone | Safari | **Compartir** → **Agregar a inicio** |

Una vez instalada, se abre en pantalla completa desde el ícono de la moneda dorada.

### Pantalla principal

Cada mitad muestra, desde el punto de vista de su jugador:

- **Nombre y marcador:** toca el nombre para editarlo; dos puntos marcan los juegos ganados.
- **Fichas y contadores:** el botón "+" queda fijo a la izquierda; la fila se desplaza hacia el lado y muestra flechas cuando hay más elementos.
- **Puntos de vida:** número grande; al tocarlo se abre la calculadora para tu propia vida.
- **Cuadro del oponente:** la vida y el nombre del rival; al tocarlo se abre la calculadora para él. Justo encima están sus fichas, con su propio "+" fijo a la derecha.
- **Botón de historial** en la esquina superior y el **cronómetro**, cuando está activado.

La barra central tiene deshacer (flecha circular), dado, moneda, carta aleatoria, fin del juego (corona de laurel) y menú (engranaje). Los íconos significan lo mismo vistos desde cualquier lado.

## Updating / Atualizar / Actualizar

Replace `index.html` and bump the cache name in `sw.js` (for example `goatcalc-v2` → `goatcalc-v3`). Installed copies update the next time they open online.
